Decision 005 · Session 4 outcomes + Round 1 closed Most recent · expanded

2026-06-11 · decision-005 · status logged
Session 4 (Juan, Austin facilitating; Farzana Nasser, Chris Nella as Chameleon voices) closes out Round 1 at 10 of 10 voices across 4 sessions. The patterns held — 9 of 10 Chameleons don't actively use their profile, all customize per pitch, bio slides remain the substitute. Four new ideas surfaced, several existing ideas refined.

Newly aligned from Session 4:
  • Custom intake forms per Chameleon. Each Chameleon writes their own qualifying questions on the contact form, demonstrating how they think about scoping. Distinct from the standard pre-populated fields. Logged as Idea 30.
  • Quarterly maintenance reminders + growth-hack analytics. Quarterly nudge for stale profiles paired with analytics showing what's driving traffic and matches. Two-sided incentive: keep your profile updated AND see the payoff. Logged as Idea 31.
  • Expert Network integration. Trusted experts get the same profile system, removing the “two layers of Chameleon” feel clients currently experience. Logged as Idea 32.
  • Local override of central data. The profile pulls defaults from the central source (COS → Core), but each profile's edits live locally. UX mechanic refining Idea 26. Logged as Idea 33.

Critical refinements — what Session 4 sharpened:
  • Idea 29 (Automated post-engagement testimonial collection): must include Chameleon-approval gate. Farzana and Chris Nella explicit: the system cannot autonomously email clients. It notifies the Chameleon, the Chameleon approves (or modifies, or skips) before the email goes out. This is not optional. Updates the idea's recommendation; rescore stays A.
  • Peer endorsements — internal-only, not public. S3 endorsed peer endorsements as testimonial copy on the profile. S4 explicitly disagreed: peer endorsements have no credibility to clients, only to internal team-builders. Refinement: peer endorsements live in the admin / matching tools (when a Chameleon is recommending another), not on the public profile. Public testimonials come from clients only. Not a contradiction — a sharper placement. Best-practices updated.
  • Idea 02 (Peer-to-peer video testimonials): stronger caveat. Video production varies too much without AI-assisted normalization to be safe to default-on. Stays opt-in; flag the production-quality concern in the implementation.
  • Idea 26 (Master + tailored children): Farzana's “hub for bio slides” phrase is the Chameleon-native framing. Adopt for internal explanation.

Methodology note — Round 1 complete: Four sessions in group format (4, 5, 5, 4 attendees). 1:1 was never viable. We accept the tradeoff and flag it honestly in synthesis. Sample skews toward Chameleons who actively think about their own go-to-market; less represented are delivery-only and Expert Network voices. Round 2 (concept reaction) should explicitly invite one of each. Total scored ideas: 33.

Decision 004 · Session 3 outcomes + Round 1 sample expanded

2026-06-10 · 5 new ideas · 4th routing tier
2026-06-10 · decision-004 · status logged
Session 3 (Juan, Austin facilitating; Chris Baccus, Jeff Blettner, Pamela Alegria as Chameleon voices) brought us to 8 of 10 voices. Sample expanded from 8 to 10 — Session 4 tomorrow will add two more, ideally a delivery-focused practitioner and a quieter or newer Chameleon, to balance Round 1's slight skew toward go-to-market-active voices. Five new ideas scored, three existing ideas reframed with Session 3 input, and the locked best-practices doc updated to reflect new commitments.

Newly aligned from Session 3:
  • Automated post-engagement testimonial collection. Triggered after engagement completes (or 3/6-month checkpoints for retainers). Thumbs up/down → 3-sentence prompt → Chameleon moderates which display. Logged as Idea 29.
  • Master + tailored children profile system. One canonical master profile; Chameleons generate tailored derivatives for specific pitches; each child is saveable and shareable. Logged as Idea 26.
  • AI-tailored bio + profile coaching workflow. Jeff's existing manual AI workflow built into the system: input client + JD, AI generates a tailored bio from the master, AI surfaces relevant experience the Chameleon undersold. Logged as Idea 25.
  • Multi-format output (web + slide/PDF). One source of truth, multiple output formats. Web stays auto-updated and mobile-responsive; slide/PDF export covers the “polished feel” Chameleons still want. Logged as Idea 27.
  • Cohesive group/team profile assembly. Biz-dev-facing tool that compiles a unified multi-Chameleon pitch artifact instead of manual PDF compilation. Logged as Idea 28.
  • Six-week project timeline reaffirmed. Wireframes → review → handoff to web team. No change to existing arc.

Reframings — what Session 3 changed:
  • Idea 14 (Variable lead-routing tiers): fourth tier added. Chris and Pamela explicitly preferred fully centralized routing (all leads to BD, no direct routing through profile). Joins the three existing tiers (raw direct / spam-filtered / vetted+closing). Every preference across all three sessions now has a covered option.
  • External vs. Chameleon-ecosystem activity sources. Earlier best-practices anticipated pulling activity from LinkedIn / personal sites. Chris explicitly preferred keeping everything inside the Chameleon ecosystem; Pamela aligned. Updated best-practices: external ingestion supported but optional per Chameleon. Native modules can be Chameleon-only for those who prefer.
  • Activity stream motivation. Juan tested whether direct lead capture would motivate Chameleons to publish more content. Chris and Pamela said no — they preferred Collective-cohesion to individual lead gen. Updated best-practices: the activity stream's purpose is brand and credibility, not direct lead capture.
  • Idea 05 (Auto-populated logo bar): contextual annotation needed. Chris flagged the “NASCAR of unknown logos” problem — for less-known clients, small annotations (stage, employee count, industry) help prospects place a brand. Folded into Idea 5 as an implementation refinement.
  • Idea 04 (Client testimonials feature): automation layer separated into Idea 29. The base testimonial feature stays; the automated collection system is its companion.

Methodology note — Round 1 status: Three sessions done in group format (4, 5, 5 attendees). 1:1 was not viable. We adapted with smaller groups, async pre-read, async follow-up, explicit dissent solicitation in the room. The synthesis flags what we can't fully see — quiet voices that didn't push back in a room — rather than pretending we've fully heard every voice. Session 4 tomorrow closes Round 1, then Round 2 (concept reaction) begins with Austin's concept introduced.
