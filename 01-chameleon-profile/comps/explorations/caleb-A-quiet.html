Brooklyn · 2025

I work at the intersection of brand strategy and the written word. For seventeen years I've helped companies figure out what they're really trying to say, then helped them say it — in voice playbooks, naming systems, manifestos, anthems, packaging, ads, and the boring 200-word product descriptions that most brands fumble.

The work usually starts the same way. A leadership team that knows the brand has drifted but can't quite articulate why. I get on the call, listen, then come back with the language. Sometimes it's a complete voice overhaul. Sometimes it's just one sentence that lets everyone breathe out and say that's the one.

Selected Work

Three recent engagements, in long-form.

Mastercard · 2024
Voice Strategy

Rebuilding tone across seven product surfaces.

Mastercard's product organization had grown faster than its voice. Seven different teams were writing in seven different registers. We wrote the unifying voice document, then ran a six-week embed where I rewrote alongside their copy leads.

Outcome: Voice document still in use 18 months later; measurable lift on card-comparison flows.

Stripe · 2023
Brand Messaging

A second-person platform voice for developers.

Stripe asked us to refine the way they speak to developers without losing the way they speak to founders. The answer wasn't pick one — it was build a system where both could coexist in the same surface.

Outcome: Adopted across Docs, Atlas, and Stripe Press.

Patagonia · 2022
Editorial Language

A long-form brand book and voice playbook.

Patagonia has one of the strongest brand voices in market. The challenge wasn't to invent it — it was to codify it for an organization that had grown past the founder's range.

Outcome: Currently the operating doc for editorial, marketing, and product content.
