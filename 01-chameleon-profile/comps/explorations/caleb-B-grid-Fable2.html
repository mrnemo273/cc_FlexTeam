002 · About

Where strategy meets the written word.

I work at the intersection of brand strategy and the written word. For seventeen years I've helped companies figure out what they're really trying to say, then helped them say it.

The work usually starts the same way. A leadership team that knows the brand has drifted but can't quite articulate why. Sometimes it's a complete voice overhaul. Sometimes it's just one sentence that lets everyone breathe out and say that's the one.
