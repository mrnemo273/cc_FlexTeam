Chapter 002 · About

Where strategy meets the written word.

I work at the intersection of brand strategy and the written word. For seventeen years I've helped companies figure out what they're really trying to say, then helped them say it.

The work usually starts the same way. A leadership team that knows the brand has drifted but can't quite articulate why. Sometimes it's a complete voice overhaul. Sometimes it's just one sentence that lets everyone breathe out and say that's the one.

Chapter 004 · Selected work

Three recent engagements.

Mastercard · Voice Strategy · 2024

Rebuilding tone across seven product surfaces.

Mastercard's product organization had grown faster than its voice. We wrote the unifying voice document, then ran a six-week embed where I rewrote the highest-traffic surfaces alongside their copy leads.

Stripe · Brand Messaging · 2023

A second-person platform voice for developers.

Stripe asked us to refine the way they speak to developers without losing the way they speak to founders. The answer was build a system where both could coexist in the same surface.
