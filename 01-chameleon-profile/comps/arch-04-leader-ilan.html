Background

From The Early Days Of Online Luxury To The Next Generation Of DTC.

I've spent the past twenty years focused on retail innovation, with an emphasis on DTC leadership, digital strategy, and merchandising. In the early online days, I launched and managed ecommerce for many of the leading fashion and luxury brands at Neiman Marcus — Michael Kors, Valentino, Salvatore Ferragamo, David Yurman, Juicy Couture. I later joined YOOX to start and run their Monobrand Division globally.

My consulting work is focused on interim leadership — melding into the executive team of global brands to scale and profitably grow their DTC business. I've held interim roles for Cartier, MAC Cosmetics, Francesca's, Love Wellness, and Theory.

I'm also an advisor to startups and digitally native brands including Stantt, Snowe, MouthMedia Network, LiveStory, and Refinery29.
