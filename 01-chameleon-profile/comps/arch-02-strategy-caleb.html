About

Where Strategy Meets The Written Word.

I work at the intersection of brand strategy and the written word. For seventeen years I've helped companies figure out what they're really trying to say, then helped them say it — in voice playbooks, naming systems, manifestos, anthems, packaging, ads, and the boring 200-word product descriptions most brands fumble.

The work usually starts the same way: a leadership team that knows the brand has drifted but can't quite articulate why. I get on the call, listen, then come back with the language. Sometimes it's a complete voice overhaul. Sometimes it's just one sentence that lets everyone breathe out and say that's the one.

Before Chameleon Collective I was in-house at three agencies and one venture-backed brand. The agency years taught me how to write to a brief; the brand year taught me what the brief was hiding. Both are useful.

Practice Areas

What I Do, In Three Areas.

01

Brand Voice Systems

A complete voice playbook — principles, tone, vocabulary, do's and don'ts, examples across product surfaces. Usually a 6–10 week engagement, ending with a document the team uses for years.

02

Positioning & Messaging

The earlier, harder problem — what's this brand actually selling, and to whom, and why now? Outputs a positioning thesis, the elevator pitch, the value-proposition hierarchy.

03

Naming & Identity Language

Product names, feature names, sub-brands, taglines. Sometimes a full naming engagement; sometimes just a sprint when an in-house team is stuck.

Selected Work · Three Recent Engagements

The Long-Form Of It.

Mastercard·Voice Strategy·2024

Rebuilding tone across seven product surfaces.

Mastercard's product organization had grown faster than its voice. Seven different teams were writing about the same product in seven different registers. We came in to write the unifying voice document, then ran a six-week embed where I wrote alongside their copy leads to actually rewrite the highest-traffic surfaces.

Stripe·Brand Messaging·2023

A second-person platform voice for developers.

Stripe asked us to help refine the way they speak to developers without losing the way they speak to founders. The answer wasn't pick one — it was build a system where both could coexist in the same surface without sounding split-brained.

Patagonia·Editorial Language·2022

A long-form brand book and voice playbook.

Patagonia has one of the strongest brand voices in market. The challenge wasn't to invent it — it was to codify it for an organization that had grown past the founder's range. Currently the operating doc for editorial, marketing, and product content.
