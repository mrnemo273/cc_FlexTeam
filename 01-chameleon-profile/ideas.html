Patterns across 33 ideas

Six strategic patterns the 33 scored ideas roll up into — the elevated story of what we're collectively building, beyond any one card. Plus a quantified read of the grade distribution, the topical clusters, and how the discovery curve flattened across four sessions.

Six strategic patterns — the shape of what we're building
Pattern 01
6 ideas

The page acquires memory.

The profile stops being a static brochure. It holds a default state, tailors per pitch, outputs in multiple formats, and lets each Chameleon override locally without rewriting the master.

25 · 26 · 27 · 28 · 30 · 33

Pattern 02
5 ideas

Defaults do the work.

Eight or nine of ten Chameleons will never touch their page. It has to show up presentable, current, and matched to brief without any editing. Customization is a power-user layer on top.

01 · 08 · 09 · 11 · 13

Pattern 03
9 ideas

Trust travels through clients.

The Collective sets the table; the credibility that closes business comes from people who paid us. Client testimonials, named projects, outcome data, off-page social proof. Peer endorsements live internal-only.

04 · 05 · 29 · 02 · 10 · 20 · 24 · 22 · 12

Pattern 04
4 ideas

Lead routing is opt-in, not enforced.

The most contested theme of Round 1, resolved by giving every Chameleon their own routing posture — direct, vetted, variable, or fully centralized. No single rule. Each Chameleon chooses.

03 · 06 · 07 · 14

Pattern 05
4 ideas

Findability becomes a first-class feature.

Today's filters (Leaders / Delivery / Recruiters) don't match how clients actually search. Curated tagging, conversational search, and a bifurcated structure replace them. The right Chameleon should be findable by the words a client actually uses.

15 · 16 · 21 · 23

Pattern 06
5 ideas

The system stays current on its own.

Availability auto-updates from real signals. Quarterly nudges surface stale profiles. Analytics show what's working. The Expert Network shares the same system. Maintenance becomes a feature of the product, not the user.
