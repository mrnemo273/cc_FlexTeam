The Brief

Juan-Carlos Morales

The Chameleon profile page is a decision-support tool for the prospect who lands on it. Its job is to help that person decide whether to reach out.

Working Note
Brief locked after the framing shifted from “Chameleons own and edit their pages” to “the page works for the human who lands on it.” See Decision 001 for the reasoning.

What we're proposing

Rebuild the Chameleon profile experience around the moment that matters most — when a prospective client arrives. The page becomes a decision-support tool that helps them decide whether to reach out.

The Chameleon is the source of truth for what's on it; who maintains it is a downstream question (could be the Chameleon, could be team-curated, could be hybrid). Underlying data — skills, industries, case studies — feeds other site experiences (matching, search, featuring) without crowding the page itself.

The current SEO-driven page can persist for crawler purposes. This is a new experience layered alongside it.

Why now

  1. Today's profile pages are failing the human moment — tag walls optimized for crawlers, not for prospects.
  2. Austin built his own version (austindillman.chameleoncollective.com) to fill the gap. The concept exists in the wild and proves a richer version is reachable.
  3. Collective Capitalism, Ch. 7 (“Find Your People”), frames sharing the vision as foundational to collective formation — the profile is where each Chameleon's vision meets the world.

What we're asking from Chameleons

This is a listening exercise, not a sell. We are not seeking validation for Austin's concept. We're seeking real signal on what would make a profile useful — including the dissenting view that maybe we shouldn't invest in this at all.

  • ListenRound 1 need discovery. Chameleons share pain and aspiration. No concept shown.
  • ReactRound 2 concept test. Austin's concept introduced. Honest reactions captured.
  • RefineSurface constraints — brand, privacy, IP, maintenance reality.
  • BuildIdentify Chameleons who'd help if the project advances. Not asked in Round 1.
Open Question
Do we have explicit buy-in from the Leadership Council to spend Round 1 time on this? Informally yes, formally not raised yet. Worth confirming before scheduling begins.
