UX · DETAIL 04 · THE CHAMELEON ADMIN

Your back office.

Every other detail page shows what the client sees. This one is what the Chameleon controls. Eight surfaces in a single editor — with a CC Intelligence layer feeding community-driven suggestions across all of them. You manage your Identity (including a Bio Library of context-specific variants), Content, Testimonials, Marketing assets, Findability + Availability, Artifacts (the outbox of every warm-pitch URL and asset you've generated), and read your Insights here. Desktop-first, because this is where the real work happens. Mobile companions for the quick stuff: toggling availability, sending a testimonial request, sharing a quote card, checking who viewed your warm-pitch.

Flow — intelligence on top, one editor, every surface
01
ADMIN · CC INTELLIGENCE

The Collective talks back.

A community-driven suggestion engine that turns the Collective into a quiet advisor. Anonymous aggregate patterns — what other Chameleons are tagging, writing, requesting, sharing — surface as specific, dismissable suggestions across every admin surface. Not a feed of "engagement," not a dopamine drip. A focused list of "here's what's working for people like you, and here's the one-click way to try it." Lives on its own page (the inbox), and also peeks into every other admin section as a small banner. This is the Collective Capitalism principle of Sharing built directly into the product — the more Chameleons use the system, the smarter it gets for everyone.

Used by
Every Chameleon · ongoing · the entry point when you open admin
Data sources
Anonymous aggregate from the Collective · match logs · your own activity history · external mentions
Feeds
Identity / Content / Findability / Marketing / Testimonials — each suggestion deep-links into the right editor
Embodies
Collective Capitalism's Sharing principle — the platform gets smarter for everyone the more it's used
02
ADMIN · IDENTITY

Who you are, where you've been.

The basics that every other surface inherits from: name, photo, role, headline, bio, and a few key stats. Edited in one place, then mirrored into Profile, Card, Bio Module, and Pill. A right-side preview rail shows each surface updating live as you type. Mobile companion exists for the one thing Chameleons ask for most often on their phone — a quick headline edit before a meeting.

Used by
Every Chameleon · once on setup, then for keep-warm edits before pitches
Edits
Name · photo · role · location · headline · bio · key stats
Lives in
Profile · Card · Bio Module · Pill — single source of truth
Auto-fills
Headline → search result snippets · Bio → SEO meta description · Stats → schema.org Person markup
03
ADMIN · CONTENT

Case studies, articles, testimonials.

The substance behind the headline — the work you've actually done. A library view shows every piece you've published: case studies (long-form work), articles (POV writing), and testimonials (client quotes). Each has a status (draft / live), a performance score (how often it's referenced in matches), and one-tap edit. The editor itself is a rich text surface with a metadata sidebar where you tag the brand, capability, and outcome — the same fields that feed Curated findability and the Bio Module carousel.

Used by
Chameleons after a project closes · before pitching a new brief
Edits
Case studies · articles · testimonials · brand metadata · capability tags
Feeds
Bio Module carousel · Curated findability · SEO Article schema · CC Find match reasoning
Replaces
PDFs traded in DMs · the case study "I'll send you in a sec" loop
04
ADMIN · TESTIMONIALS + ENDORSEMENTS

Other people's words for you.

Two related surfaces in one section. Testimonials are client quotes — long-form, on a project, attributable to a real human. Endorsements are peer-validated skill claims, like LinkedIn's "endorsed for X" but with stronger signal (only people you've actually worked with can endorse, and the system surfaces them when the brief calls for that skill). A request flow lets you ask for either, with a default template that's polite, specific, and easy to say yes to. An automated engine watches who you've collaborated with recently and proactively suggests "Sarah Patel was your client on PWC. Want to send her a testimonial request?"

Used by
Chameleons after a project closes · or when CC Intelligence flags a warm signal worth asking
Edits
Testimonials (request, accept, display, retire) · endorsement skills (claim → wait for peer validation)
Feeds
Profile testimonials section · Bio Module quote carousel · CC Find match reasoning · SEO Person schema
Different from LinkedIn
Only past collaborators can endorse — spam-free · CC suggests who to ask — you don't hunt manually
05
ADMIN · MARKETING TOOLKIT

How the work gets out.

Two surfaces that work together: a community feed (what other Chameleons are sharing right now — LinkedIn posts, talks, articles, op-eds — ready to repost, quote, or comment on) and an asset generator (pick any case study or article from your Content Library, get four export-ready formats: quote card for social, LinkedIn post draft, one-pager PDF, email signature snippet). This is how a Chameleon turns the work they already did into surface area for new work — without learning Figma, hiring a marketer, or staring at a blank canvas. The Collective's distribution becomes your distribution.

Used by
Chameleons who want distribution without learning Figma or hiring marketing
Generates
Quote cards (PNG) · LinkedIn drafts · One-pager PDFs · Email-signature HTML
Pulls from
Your Content Library · the Collective's share-worthy items · CC Newsroom
Embodies
The Collective's distribution = your distribution — share each other's good work
06
ADMIN · FINDABILITY + AVAILABILITY

How clients find you (and when you're open).

The bridge to Search & Discovery. Three controls in one screen: tags (curated + AI-suggested), availability (calendar with status, sync options, and auto-learn), and routing preferences (which industries / work types you actually want). Changes here propagate to all four search modes. Availability lives here because it's a findability signal, not a calendar tool — the question is "should clients see me as a match this week?" Sync from Google / iCal / Outlook is one toggle; manual entry is always available; and CC auto-learns from match acceptance patterns. Mobile companion handles the one toggle Chameleons want from the road.

Used by
Every Chameleon · ongoing · the tags evolve as work evolves
Edits
Curated tags · accept/reject AI suggestions · calendar status · industry & work-type prefs
Feeds
CC Find ranking · Filtered search facets · Curated tag pages · Routing logic for inbound briefs
Mobile delivers
One-tap availability change · the single most-asked-for mobile flow from Round 1
07
ADMIN · ARTIFACTS · YOUR OUTBOX

Everything you've sent into the world.

A unified library of every artifact the system has helped you generate — Smart Deliverable warm-pitch URLs (one-of-one landing pages BD sent on your behalf), Marketing assets (quote cards, LinkedIn drafts, PDFs, email sigs), and Profile snapshots (frozen versions shared with a specific client). Each row has type, recipient (when relevant), created date, status (live / scheduled / expired), and per-artifact views. This is the answer to "wait, what did I send Sarah three weeks ago?" and "is that warm-pitch link still alive?" Filter by type, recipient, or status. Re-share, refresh, or retire from one place. Mobile companion is a recent-activity feed — "Sarah viewed your warm-pitch 3× today."

Used by
Chameleons after they've sent something into the world — pitch, post, snapshot
Aggregates
Smart Deliverable URLs · Marketing assets (PNG, posts, PDFs, sigs) · Profile snapshots
Lifecycle
Scheduled → Live → Expiring → Expired · with one-click renew, retire, or nudge actions
Replaces
"What did I send Sarah three weeks ago?" — the scattered Notion/Drive/email hunt
08
ADMIN · INSIGHTS

What's working.

Read-only. The other three sections are where you do work; this is where you find out if the work paid off. Top-line KPIs (impressions, profile views, match rate, brief volume), trend chart over 90 days, top match categories, and a recent activity feed that names the actual surfaces clients arrived from. Mobile companion is a weekly digest — one card, three numbers, one observation. Quiet enough that you'll actually open it.

Used by
Every Chameleon · weekly · monthly · before refreshing case studies
Edits
Nothing — read-only by design. Actions deep-link to Identity / Content / Findability
Data sources
CC Find match logs · Profile page views · SEO arrivals · Curated tag-page traffic · brief response rate
Replaces
"How do I know if my profile is working?" — the question every Round 1 voice asked at least once
CLOSES THE LOOP · PROJECT 01

One editor. Eight surfaces. The Collective makes you smarter, you make the Collective smarter.

The Profile Revamp is finished when every Chameleon can sit down at this admin and shape their own findability, their own distribution, their own evidence base — all without a meeting, an email, or a ping to BD. Intelligence flags what to fix. Identity (with a Bio Library of variants per context) + Content + Testimonials are your evidence. Marketing turns that evidence into reach. Findability + Availability control when and how clients meet you. Artifacts is your outbox — every warm-pitch URL, every quote card, every snapshot tracked in one place. Insights closes the loop so Intelligence gets smarter for everyone.

This is Collective Capitalism's Sharing principle operationalized: the more Chameleons use the platform, the better the suggestions get for every Chameleon. The system has compounding returns to the Collective itself.
