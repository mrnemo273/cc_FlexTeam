### Summary

Discussions on platform profile usability and centralization were conducted to optimize lead management and content customization strategies.

**Current profile limitations identified**  
Existing profile pages suffer from poor discoverability and outdated content. Users require more flexible, modular layouts to better tailor professional presentations to specific client needs.

**System integration and structure**  
A transition to a centralized database will enable more robust data management. Decision made to implement a local override mechanism allowing users to customize data pulled from the central system.

**Lead management and discoverability**  
Enhanced search functionality and custom lead intake forms were identified as critical features. Analytics will be used to incentivize members to maintain accurate and updated profile information.

### Details

* **Initiative Overview and Purpose**: Juan Morales and Austin Dillman explained that they are conducting a series of interviews with a 10-person group to gather feedback for a grassroots growth committee initiative. The goal is to identify and address low-hanging fruit to help members—referred to as "chameleons"—sell services better, specifically focusing on the website profile pages which currently suffer from stale content and usability issues ([00:02:18](#00:02:18)).

* **Current Profile Challenges and Usability**: Participants discussed that the current website profiles are not frequently used because they are difficult to find, often contain outdated information, and suffer from poor mobile layout design ([00:01:37](#00:01:37)) ([00:05:07](#00:05:07)). Chris Nella noted having to resort to an external Google Slides document because the existing profile was ineffective, while Farzana Nasser pointed out that the current process for updating content on the Client Operating System (COS) is cumbersome and painful ([00:05:07](#00:05:07)) ([00:06:43](#00:06:43)).

* **Desire for Control and Customization**: Farzana Nasser and Chris Nella expressed a strong desire for more flexibility and control over their profiles ([00:07:40](#00:07:40)). They suggested that the page should act as a hub for "bio slides" that allow members to customize skills, add logos, and tailor content for specific client needs without needing to overhaul the entire site ([00:06:43](#00:06:43)).

* **System Integration and Centralization**: Juan Morales mentioned that the organization is transitioning from the current Client Operating System to a new tool called "Core," which will serve as the central source of truth for member information ([00:14:54](#00:14:54)). Chris Nella proposed that while profiles should pull data from this central system by default, there should be a mechanism to allow for local, real-time overwriting of content to tailor the presentation ([00:13:17](#00:13:17)).

* **Flexibility and Archetypes**: Austin Dillman and Juan Morales discussed the need for profiles to adapt to different client types, such as startups or private equity firms. They proposed using "archetypes" and modular "Lego blocks" to allow members to selectively display content relevant to their specific roles, such as strategy or design, without breaking the site's overall consistency ([00:12:28](#00:12:28)) ([00:15:39](#00:15:39)).

* **Content Philosophy and Conciseness**: Chris Nella emphasized a preference for presenting a personality-driven and succinct overview of their working philosophy rather than overly long, detailed biographies ([00:16:38](#00:16:38)). Farzana Nasser agreed that the page should provide enough copy blocks to allow individuals to decide how to best lead with their own style and professional approach ([00:17:33](#00:17:33)).

* **Multimedia and Credibility**: Austin Dillman explored the idea of incorporating video or audio to better showcase personality. Farzana Nasser suggested that video might pose a production challenge, proposing instead that linking to speaking engagements, podcasts, or published articles would better serve to add credibility ([00:18:21](#00:18:21)).

* **Testimonials and Validation**: Juan Morales suggested implementing a client endorsement system similar to LinkedIn ([00:20:07](#00:20:07)). Farzana Nasser agreed on the value of client endorsements but cautioned that any automation of the request process must include a manual approval step for the engagement lead, as the timing of asking for recommendations is critical and depends on the specific client relationship ([00:21:08](#00:21:08)).

* **Internal Peer Feedback**: Austin Dillman asked if members should include testimonials from other chameleons regarding their work. Farzana Nasser and Chris Nella agreed that such feedback is highly valuable for internal vetting and team building but noted it has little value for external clients, who are primarily focused on professional qualifications ([00:21:50](#00:21:50)).

* **Analytics and Search Functionality**: Chris Nella and Farzana Nasser discussed the current lack of discoverability on the site, noting that existing search filters are ineffective. Austin Dillman acknowledged that search functionality is a critical gap and suggested using AI or better data structuring to allow clients to filter by industry, title, or capability rather than just by person ([00:24:37](#00:24:37)) ([00:27:14](#00:27:14)).

* **Maintenance and Incentives**: To combat stale content, participants discussed potential maintenance strategies, including quarterly reminders or a yearly cleanup period ([00:28:08](#00:28:08)). Juan Morales and Austin Dillman suggested that providing analytics on lead generation could incentivize members to keep their profiles updated and rich with content ([00:28:55](#00:28:55)) ([00:32:00](#00:32:00)).

* **Lead Management Preferences**: Austin Dillman asked how members prefer to receive leads. Chris Nella indicated a preference for vetting leads personally, while Juan Morales noted that the desire to handle leads varies, with roughly half of the members preferring to manage them independently and others opting out ([00:30:28](#00:30:28)).

* **Customized Lead Intake**: Chris Nella proposed creating brief, custom intake forms to qualify leads and demonstrate operational thoughtfulness ([00:33:29](#00:33:29)). Juan Morales confirmed that the transition to cloud infrastructure makes previously impossible features, such as custom intake forms, now feasible ([00:34:45](#00:34:45)).

* **Project Timeline and Next Steps**: Juan Morales stated that they have a six-week timeline for this initiative, with this discussion marking week one. Austin Dillman and Juan Morales plan to compile feedback to create wireframes, which will be shared with the participants via a dedicated Slack channel, before handing the designs over to the web team for implementation ([00:35:26](#00:35:26)).

* **Future Automation Vision**: Austin Dillman and Juan Morales highlighted a broader vision to improve promotion, such as automatically surfacing profiles to the homepage when members have capacity or pulling in recent social media posts to keep profiles fresh ([00:37:17](#00:37:17)).
