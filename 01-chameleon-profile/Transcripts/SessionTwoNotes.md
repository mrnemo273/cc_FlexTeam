### Summary

Chameleon members aligned on profile page enhancements for lead generation via collaborative design and system integration.

**Improving Profile Page Strategy**  
Increased awareness of automated profile pages drives a strategic pivot toward lead generation instead of maintaining general social profiles.

**Enhancing Profile Functionality**  
Integrated case studies, media, and availability tracking will clearly position the platform as a cohesive team rather than a traditional agency.

**Developing New Profile Wireframes**  
Collaborative wireframe development via a dedicated channel establishes a framework for implementation within the 6 to 12 month website replatforming schedule.

### Decisions

## Aligned

* **Profile page purpose focused on business** The profile pages will be purpose-built exclusively for lead generation and business facilitation, prioritizing this objective over social presence or general company information.

* **Opt-in variable lead vetting process** The lead vetting process via profile contact forms will be variable and opt-in, allowing individual chameleons to choose between receiving leads directly or having them vetted by the sales and marketing team.

* **Positioning strategy centered on team hiring** The messaging strategy for individual profiles will prioritize the position of 'Hire your team, not your agency' to clearly communicate the business model to potential clients.
